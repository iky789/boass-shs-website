MISSION

Boa Amponsem Senior High School seeks to provide relevant education to allstudents at the second-cycle level, creating a propitious environment for their spiritual and moral development. The school aims to establish a framework in which each student can fully develop their talents while respecting those of others

VISION

The school is committed to fostering, maintaining, and implementing sound educational practices that align with Ghana's educational policies. This commitment is intended to enable Boa Amponsem Senior High School to gain international recognition and to equip students to face the challenges of a rapidly changing world, maximizing opportunities for self-development and service to humanity.
